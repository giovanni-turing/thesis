The constraint

A week of bursts — too fast to react to

episode = contiguous minutes above the p99 image rate (224 img/s), extended to the surrounding median crossings → 18 episodes · onset = baseline → peak ramp time

Median onset 6.5 min, fastest 1 min — inside a single observation lag. Cold starts run 15 s – 5 min: for the fast half, the budget onset − cold-start ≤ 0. At an issued forecast lead of L=30 s reactive control fails 9 of 18 episodes. The only way out: capacity that exists before the burst — which requires predicting the image rate. →

The bet

Forecast the image rate — spend the lead building capacity

burst3 (1-min onset) at lead L = 30 s — ledger-verified schedules from the converter runs; capacity = workers × ρ (15 img/s per worker); demand shown at 5-s resolution (the scheduler consumes its 15-s means) · hover for workers & rates
1 · zero-shot foundation forecasters
Chronos-2 · Toto-2 · TimesFM-2.0 forecast the 15-s image rate; the p90 quantile path is the planning signal — empirically calibrated (coverage 0.88–0.90).
2 · issue-time honesty
A forecast for time t is usable only if issued ≥ L s earlier — L = the replica cold start. No hindsight anywhere in the loop.
3 · one law, two information sets
C = ceil(demand / ρ), 3-min hysteresis — identical for both policies. Forecast sees ahead; reactive sees the trailing bin. That is the entire difference.

1 · the characterization
First to quantify the modality decoupling (R² = 0.12) and frame per-pool demand as forecasting targets — not a reactive load signal.
2 · the forecast benchmark
First provisioning-framed zero-shot TSFM benchmark on a production multimodal trace: foundation models win 20/20 cells; the p90 edge calibrates to 0.88–0.90 at provisioning cadence.
3 · the silicon anchor
AIC's first consumer-GPU encoder dataset (RTX 5090, locked clocks, PR #1312) — attention within ≈3%; complete vLLM vision tower within 12.8% over N ≤ 64.
4 · the platform + the knee
A calibrated two-pool simulator with per-pool attribution: saturation knees an order of magnitude apart; the largest burst breaks each pool in a structurally different way.
5 · the closed loop — the contribution proper
The p90 path holds the SLA on every covered window at every issued lead at 0.62× the deployable static fleet — and in the reactive-favorable post-hoc floor envelope, the cheapest every-window-passing floor costs 1.20× the forecast (a symmetric forecast+floor family was not swept). The band shape is load-bearing, not point accuracy.

Scope, stated plainly: simulation-derived, one production trace, one infrastructure model; costs are separated by declared readiness mechanics; the p90 band is calibrated to ~90% coverage.
