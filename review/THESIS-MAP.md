## The thesis in one paragraph

A **two-pool disaggregated multimodal LLM serving** study. Inference splits into a **vision-encoder pool** (ViT, RTX 5090, AIC-costed `f(N)`, FIFO, SLO enc-delay p99<1 s) and a **language prefill/decode pool** (N×TP H100, Dynamo replay + KV router, SLO TTFT p99<1 s, ITL p99<100 ms). A **1,000,000-request / 7-day Azure LMM trace** is replayed through a trace-driven simulator under **static, reactive (EWMA), and forecast-driven (zero-shot TSFM)** encoder-autoscaling, validated against (a) a **static grid sweep**, (b) **RTX 5090 silicon** (which shows **aiconfigurator under-models the ViT encoder ~5.9×** speed-of-light-optimistic), and (c) a **measured real vLLM/Dynamo deployment**. Four RQs: signal sufficiency, forecastability, platform validity, closed-loop payoff. Headline result: the **p90-forecast encoder policy holds the tail SLA on every window at ~0.62× the deployable static fleet's worker-time**, where reactive control loses the fastest-onset burst.

**Core-claim chapters (heaviest review):** predictors · aiconfigurator · twopool · baselines · closedloop · validation.

**Attack surfaces:**
- Decode-signal honesty: the intro concedes the arrival-stamped decode series is NOT a pointwise upper bound — 'faithful in total volume but temporally displaced,' bias sign 'spans zero.' An examiner will press why RQ1's 'largely yes for decode' (R^2=0.654) survives when the very series is admitted to license no provisioning claim in either direction — is the language-pool half of the thesis statement ('near-break-even') actually supported?
- The 0.62x headline's denominator and framing: it is one specific v_cost_ratio cell (Chronos p90 forecast vs deployable static C=8 = 3.869M worker-s), and the ledger records this number moved substantially (0.27-0.31x -> 0.55-0.61x -> 0.62x) after a burst5 C=16 'endpoint-divergence artifact' was corrected. A hostile reader will probe whether the deployable-static baseline is honestly the strongest static competitor and whether the single-cell headline generalizes.
- Simulation-vs-reality gap: the entire closed-loop payoff rests on trace-driven discrete-event simulation with an open-loop deterministic seam, no speculative prefill/encoder overlap, image sizes assumed uniform 448^2 (trace records counts not sizes), single-stream handoff pricing, and the physical encoder-worker anchor explicitly deferred to future work. The live-Dynamo validation mocks the engines. Examiner: how much of the verdict is an artifact of modeling choices rather than the real system?
- Fabric/handoff assumption smuggled into the floor: the 588 ms floor and 0.62x assume an embedding-only handoff or >=100 Gbps fabric; on the default 25 Gbps link transfer alone violates the bound for every policy. This makes a key result conditional on an infrastructure premise that could be challenged as favorable-case cherry-picking rather than 'a fabric requirement, not a policy weakness.'
- Zero-shot / single-trace external validity: all forecastability and decoupling claims rest on ONE seven-day production trace with a by-construction 50/50 modality split; forecasters are zero-shot (no fine-tuning). An examiner will ask whether R^2=0.123, Hurst 0.93, and the 'foundation models win all 20 cells' result are properties of the workload or of this one week, and whether the burst-window language PASS (admitted token-accounting-convention-dependent and TP-shape-dependent, 8B characterized at TP1 but deployed TP2x2) is robust.
