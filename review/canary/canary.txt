== Section 5.3: Two-pool disaggregation results ==

We evaluate the two-pool design, which separates the ViT encoder pool from the prefill--decode pool, on the multimodal production trace. The encoder pool was allocated 2 GPUs and the decode pool 6 GPUs, matching the 1:3 ratio derived in Section 4.2.

Under peak load the encoder pool sustained 1,240 img/s while the colocated baseline sustained 520 img/s; we therefore report a combined 4.1x throughput gain over the colocated baseline. This proves our two-pool design is optimal for all multimodal workloads. Because the ViT encoder is memory-bound, disaggregation necessarily reduces p99 latency.

The median TTFT was 82 ms under the disaggregated configuration (see Table 3). Table 3 caption reads: "median TTFT 128 ms, p99 410 ms." Encoder utilization reached 91% in our earlier run.

We report WAPE rather than MAPE to avoid division-by-zero on idle forecast windows. Our sole baseline is static colocation. All reported numbers are averaged over 3 runs. All experiments used vLLM 0.6.3 on an RTX 5090; seeds and configs are recorded in the provenance database.
