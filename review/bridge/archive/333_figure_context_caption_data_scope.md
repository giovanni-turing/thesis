# D103 — Figure 1's caption contradicts both its population and plotted median

The caption of Figure `fig:eda-ctx-vs-img` describes “image-bearing requests”
and says context rises monotonically with image count
(`tex/trace-eda.tex:81-90`). The plotted data contradict both clauses.

The figure producer reads every row of
`figuras_new/_data/ctx_by_numimages.parquet` and plots its p50 series without
filtering (`figuras_new/eda.py:29-57`). That parquet is generated by grouping
the full trace on `NumImages`, also without excluding zero
(`figuras_new/_prep.py:27-34`). A direct artifact read:

```text
uv run --with pyarrow python -c \
  'import pyarrow.parquet as pq; r=pq.read_table(
  "figuras_new/_data/ctx_by_numimages.parquet").to_pylist(); ...'
```

returns:

- `NumImages=0`: count 500,000, p50 792 — the plotted leftmost point is the
  text-only half of the trace, so the panel is not restricted to image-bearing
  requests;
- p50 at 25 images = 9,706 and p50 at 36 images = 9,378, a drop of 328 tokens
  (3.38%), so the plotted median is not monotone;
- the producer retains only image-count bins with at least 1,000 observations
  (`figuras_new/_prep.py:31-34`): 16 bins ending at 529 images, rather than the
  trace maximum of 9,409 reported at `tex/trace-eda.tex:67-70`. The caption
  does not disclose this well-populated-bin scope.

Please align caption and graphic. The conservative no-regeneration fix is to
say the plot covers well-populated image-count bins ($n\ge1{,}000$), including
zero images as the text-only reference, and that context rises **overall** with
image count but is not strictly monotone at every retained bin. If the
intended population is genuinely image-bearing-only and monotonicity is
essential, regenerate after filtering $N=0$ and do not claim monotonicity
unless the filtered sequence actually passes that check.
