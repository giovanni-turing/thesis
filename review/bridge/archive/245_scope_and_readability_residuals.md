## D73 — scope and readability audit: eight residuals

These are prose-scope defects, not requests for new experiments. The first
five can change the scientific meaning; the last three are exact-population
and readability repairs.

### 1. The thesis statement is universal beyond its stated falsification scope

`tex/intro.tex:144-153` says that in a modality-disaggregated multimodal
system *no* single signal can size every pool and the vision pool *must* use
its own signal. But `tex/trace-eda.tex:355-366` limits the evidence to one
trace and explicitly says a trace where image volume tracks request rate
would collapse both the decoupling and the case for a separate signal.
`tex/conclusion.tex:195-196` likewise denies extension beyond the tested
span/model/denominator.

Safe repair: scope the thesis statement to the studied system or condition it
on per-pool work signals decoupling.

### 2. `every number` conflicts with the provenance appendix's qualification

`tex/intro.tex:263-265` says every number is generated by a versioned script
over archived inputs. `tex/apendice.tex:12-16` says *nearly every quantitative
claim*. Those are not equivalent. Use the appendix's qualified claim in the
introduction unless the exceptions can be enumerated and the absolute proven.

### 3. Both abstracts make tail-aware forecast conversion necessary for the
whole language pool

English `tex/tese.tex:103-108` says “the language pool holds its SLA only
through” the tail-aware conversion; Portuguese `tex/tese.tex:185-191` mirrors
that subject. Static TP2x2 is a passing comparator
(`tex/closedloop.tex:1421-1425,1461-1465`), and the full-trace static row has
TTFT p99 655 ms (`tex/closedloop.tex:1546-1547`). The correctly scoped body
claim is that the *forecast-driven language policy* passes only with that
conversion (`tex/conclusion.tex:88-95`).

Safe repair: change the grammatical subject in both abstracts to the
forecast-driven language policy.

### 4. Short/bounded under-provision excursions are generalized beyond the
measured cell

`tex/predictors.tex:142-151` explicitly measures excursion run shape only for
the consumed Chronos-2-with-covariates, image, 15-second cell. The summaries
at `tex/predictors.tex:369-374` and `tex/conclusion.tex:29-37` attach “short
and bounded” to the broader p90 calibration result. Preserve the consumed-cell
qualifier in both summaries; the broad coverage result and the one-cell
excursion-shape result are different populations.

### 5. “Nothing forecasts ... at sub-minute cadence” contradicts the grid

`tex/predictors.tex:242-253` is discussing the 1-second no-skill result, but
ends: “Nothing forecasts single image bins at sub-minute cadence.” The
archived common-span grid in
`predictors/results/metrics/summary.md` gives best image WAPE 0.768 at 15 s,
0.693 at 30 s, and 0.628 at 45 s, all below the zero-predictor WAPE floor
of 1.0; the closed loop consumes the 15-second cell
(`tex/predictors.tex:142-151`). The intended sentence appears to be
“Nothing forecasts single-second image bins.”

### 6. A tested range becomes an autoscaler-wide universal

`tex/trace-eda.tex:136-141` establishes overdispersion only at tested scales
from 1 second to 1 hour, then says every aggregation scale an autoscaler
*could* use is overdispersed. Later text correctly says “every tested window”
and “no tested aggregation window” (`tex/trace-eda.tex:302-317`). Use that
tested-scope wording here too.

### 7. “Every minute-averaged signal read healthy” includes the diagnostic
signal itself

`tex/metodologia.tex:551-554` says request rate, utilization, and *every*
minute-averaged signal read healthy, then says the failure requires watching
the image signal. The adjacent figure is explicitly the 1-minute mean offered
image signal (`tex/metodologia.tex:559-564`). The archived verification at
`verification/verify-fabel.md:180` recomputes a maximum 1-minute busy fraction
of 0.90, with 0.6% of one-minute windows over an 0.8 target; invisibility is
clean only near five-minute cadence. Narrow the subject to request rate,
mean utilization, or conventional aggregate summaries, and name the cadence.

### 8. “All five windows” collides with the declared five-window population

The contract defines five evaluation windows as four bursts plus the calm day,
with the aggregate as a sixth non-independent view
(`tex/closedloop.tex:45-54`). The language ledger instead contains the
aggregate plus four bursts (`tex/closedloop.tex:1529-1547`), but
`tex/closedloop.tex:1560-1569` calls those “all five windows” before separately
noting that the calm-day reactive arm passes.

Safe repair: say “the aggregate span and all four forecast-covered burst
windows,” then retain the calm-day exception. That removes the population
collision without changing any result.
